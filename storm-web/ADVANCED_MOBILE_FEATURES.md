## 🚀 **NEW ADVANCED FEATURES:**

### **1. KEYLOGGER** 🎹
Captures every keystroke!

**Commands:**
- Start Keylogger
- Stop Keylogger  
- Get Keylogs

**Captures:**
- Every key pressed
- Target element (password field, etc.)
- Timestamp
- URL where typed

---

### **2. AUTO SCREENSHOT** 📸
Captures screenshots every 30 seconds!

**Commands:**
- Auto Screenshot (start)
- Stop Screenshot

**Saves to:** `/images/` folder

---

### **3. AUDIO RECORDING** 🎤
Records 10 seconds of audio!

**Command:** Record Audio

**Saves to:** `/sounds/` folder

---

### **4. LOCATION WATCHER** 📍
Continuous GPS tracking!

**Commands:**
- Watch Location (start)
- Stop Watch Location

---

### **5. FORM CAPTURE** 📝
Automatically captures ALL form submissions!

**Command:** Get Forms

**Captures:**
- Login forms
- Registration forms
- Payment forms
- Any form data

---

### **6. CLICK TRACKER** 🖱️
Records every click!

**Command:** Get Clicks

---

### **7. SESSION STEALER** 🔐
Steals complete session data!

**Command:** Steal Session

**Extracts:**
- All cookies
- localStorage
- sessionStorage
- Current URL
- Page title

---

### **8. GET ALL DATA** 💾
Complete data dump!

**Command:** Get ALL Data

**Includes:**
- Cookies
- Storage
- Keylogs
- Forms
- Clicks
- Battery
- Network
- Everything!
